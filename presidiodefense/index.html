What We Do

Defense Capabilities

We provide specialized solutions across the full spectrum of defense and national security requirements — supporting federal customers from concept through sustainment.

01

Systems Integration

Design, integration, and deployment of complex mission systems for DoD and federal agency customers across classified and unclassified environments.

02

Cybersecurity & IT

Cyber defense, network operations, vulnerability assessment, and compliance support aligned to NIST, CMMC, and DISA STIGs across DoD networks.

03

Intelligence Support

Analytical support, open-source intelligence (OSINT), and geospatial analysis in support of DoD and IC mission requirements.

04

Logistics & Supply Chain

Defense logistics planning, supply chain risk management (SCRM), and sustainment support for mission-critical programs CONUS and OCONUS.

05

Training & Simulation

Curriculum development, instructional design, and live/virtual/constructive training solutions for military and federal law enforcement.

06

Program Management

End-to-end program management using risk management frameworks and DoD acquisition lifecycle expertise from requirements through close-out.

Who We Are

Mission-Focused.
Integrity-Driven.

Presidio Defense is a U.S.-based defense contracting firm committed to delivering high-quality services to the Department of Defense, the intelligence community, and federal civilian agencies.

Founded on the principles of integrity, accountability, and mission focus, we operate with the urgency and discipline that national security demands. Our leadership brings direct experience from military service, federal acquisition, and technology — bridging operational requirements with actionable solutions.

As a small business, we bring the agility to respond quickly and the dedication to perform at the highest levels alongside the nation's premier defense primes.
