About the Company

Mission-Focused. Integrity-Driven.

Presido Defence Group is a U.S.-based defense contracting firm committed to delivering high-quality services to the Department of Defense, intelligence community, and federal civilian agencies. Founded on the principles of integrity, accountability, and mission focus, we operate with the urgency and discipline that national security demands.

Our leadership team brings direct experience from military service, federal acquisition, and private-sector technology — giving us a unique ability to bridge operational requirements with actionable solutions. We are a small business with the agility to respond quickly and the expertise to perform at the highest levels.
